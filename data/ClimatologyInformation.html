Climatology Plugin for OpenCPN

This plugin visualises historical monthly weather and ocean patterns for voyage planning and supplies the established Climatology interface used by Weather Routing. It is not a forecast and its depth layer is not navigational bathymetry.

Dataset identity

Modern versioned bundles include dataset-manifest.json and DATASET_PROVENANCE.md in this data directory. They give the exact period, source product identifiers, processing choices, generator revision and SHA-256 checksums. The plugin version and dataset version are deliberately independent and are shown together on the About page.

Modern dataset sources

  • Wind atlas and atmospheric fields: ECMWF ERA5.
  • Sea-surface currents: NASA/JPL PO.DAAC OSCAR Final V2.
  • Tropical cyclones: NOAA/NCEI IBTrACS v04r01.
  • Sea-surface temperature: NOAA OISST v2.1.
  • Bathymetry context: GEBCO 2026.
  • ENSO classification: NOAA CPC ONI based on ERSSTv6.
  • Lightning: the observed NASA LIS/OTD HRMC v2.3.2013 layer is retained (1995–2013); precipitation is not used as a proxy for lightning.

The intended common target period is 1995-01-01 through 2024-12-31. Where an authoritative product has shorter coverage, the exact per-product period in the manifest takes precedence.

Interpretation

The wind atlas contains eight directional-sector frequencies and mean speeds plus calm and gale probabilities. The current layer contains vector components; directions are interpolated through the components rather than as wrapped angles. Missing coastal cells are not treated as zero.
